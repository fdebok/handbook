# Forward Deployed Engineer Body of Knowledge
## 前沿部署工程师知识体系手册

### English

As model capabilities advance, the key constraint in enterprise AI is no longer the models themselves, but the people and systems that turn them into measurable business outcomes. This handbook makes the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role teachable, measurable, and repeatable. It brings together ten knowledge domains, a six-stage delivery lifecycle, a five-level competency framework, practical diagnoses and remedies for twenty common challenges, and ready-to-use field templates.

### 中文

随着模型能力不断提升，企业级 AI 的关键瓶颈不再是模型本身，而是如何通过合适的人才与系统，将模型能力转化为可衡量的业务成果。本手册将前沿部署工程师（Forward Deployed Engineer，FDE）的工作沉淀为可教学、可衡量、可复制的知识体系，涵盖十个知识领域、六阶段交付生命周期、五级能力框架、二十类高频问题的诊断与解决方案，以及可直接使用的一线实践模板。
